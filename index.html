A message from Shabab Ali

KOSHISH

Try. Learn. Rise. Try Again.

"Koshish karne walon ko afsos nahi hota" doesn't mean everyone who tries automatically wins.

Failure can happen even after sincere effort. Success was never fully within human control — and that isn't a discouraging thought, it's a freeing one.

But that freedom only applies after the sincere effort — not instead of it.

So the discipline is this: give the effort, keep the patience, hold the faith, do the best possible amount of work you're capable of today — and then let the result be what it will be.

"Kamyabi aur nakaami Allah ke ikhtiyar mein hain; meri zimmedari sincere koshish, sabr, dua aur behtareen amal hai."

You may lose after trying. You may fall after giving your best. But sincere effort gives you something failure cannot take away: experience, character, and the knowledge that you did not simply surrender.
— Shabab Ali
